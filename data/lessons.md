# Course Lessons

Each lesson below has a short summary and its **key points**. A student "understood" a lesson if their explanation covers the key points correctly in their own words.

---

## L1 — Photosynthesis

**Summary:** Plants make their own food using sunlight. Inside the leaves, chlorophyll captures light energy. The plant takes in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the roots, and uses the light energy to turn them into glucose (a sugar it uses for energy and growth). Oxygen is released as a by-product.

**Key points:**
1. Plants use **light energy** (captured by **chlorophyll**) to make food.
2. The inputs are **carbon dioxide** and **water**.
3. The outputs are **glucose** and **oxygen**.
4. It happens mainly in the **leaves** (in chloroplasts).

## L2 — Newton's First Law (Inertia)

**Summary:** An object stays at rest, or keeps moving at the same speed in a straight line, unless an outside (unbalanced) force acts on it. This tendency to keep doing what it is already doing is called inertia. Heavier objects (more mass) have more inertia. This is why passengers lurch forward when a bus brakes suddenly.

**Key points:**
1. An object at rest **stays at rest**; a moving object **keeps moving at constant speed in a straight line**.
2. This continues **unless an unbalanced (external) force** acts on it.
3. This tendency is called **inertia**.
4. **More mass means more inertia.**

## L3 — Supply and Demand

**Summary:** The price of a product depends on supply (how much is available) and demand (how much people want it). When demand rises and supply stays the same, prices usually go up. When supply rises and demand stays the same, prices usually go down. The market settles at an equilibrium price where the amount supplied equals the amount demanded.

**Key points:**
1. **Higher demand** (with the same supply) usually **raises the price**.
2. **Higher supply** (with the same demand) usually **lowers the price**.
3. The **equilibrium price** is where **quantity supplied equals quantity demanded**.
4. Price acts as a **signal** that balances buyers and sellers.
